APP NAME (30 characters max)
FamSplit

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 characters max)
Split trip expenses between families. Heads settle up, then each family inside.

FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 characters max)
FamSplit makes trips with several families easy to settle.

When four or five families travel together, everyone pays for something: the hotel, the cabs, dinners, tickets. FamSplit keeps track of it all, then works out how to settle up: first between the families, then inside each family.

HOW IT WORKS
• Create a trip and add the families travelling together, with everyone in them, including children and grandparents who don't use the app.
• Anyone can add an expense: who paid, and how it is split.
• FamSplit works out what each family owes or gets back.
• Family heads settle with each other first. Then each family squares up inside, through its own head.

SPLIT IT YOUR WAY
• Per person: everyone pays the same
• Per family: each family pays the same, whatever its size
• By shares: give children a half share
• Exact amounts: type in what each person owes
• Several people paid? Enter what each one paid.

SETTLE UP WITHOUT THE MATHS
• Just a few payments between family heads
• Mark payments as paid, and see them alongside the expenses
• Pay with UPI: opens your own UPI app with the amount filled in
• Send a reminder notification to whoever owes

SHARE THE TRIP
• Sign in with Google and invite the other families with a code
• Everyone sees new expenses as they are added, with notifications
• An activity log shows who added, changed or deleted what
• Works offline and syncs when you are back online

WHEN THE TRIP IS OVER
• Finalise the trip to lock the expenses while everyone pays
• Share a PDF summary of the whole trip

FamSplit has no ads. It never handles your money: UPI payments happen in your own UPI app.
