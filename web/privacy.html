FamSplit privacy policy

Effective 13 October 2026

FamSplit is an app for splitting trip expenses between families. It is made and run by Sumit Patil, an individual developer. This page explains what information the app handles, why, and what you can do about it.

What FamSplit collects

  • Your Google account details. When you sign in with Google, FamSplit receives your name, email address and profile picture address. It uses your email to recognise you; it does not get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.
  • What you put into trips. Trip names, family names, the names of the people on a trip, expenses (what they were for, the amounts, who paid and how they are split), payments marked as paid, and UPI IDs if someone chooses to add one.
  • A record of changes. For shared trips, FamSplit keeps a log of who added, changed or deleted expenses and payments, so that everyone on the trip can see what happened.
  • A notification address for your phone. If you allow notifications, FamSplit stores a token that lets it send notifications to your device.

What FamSplit does not collect

  • Your location, contacts, photos or files.
  • Bank, card or payment details. FamSplit never handles money: "Pay with UPI" only opens your own UPI app with the details filled in.
  • Advertising or tracking data. There are no ads and no analytics tools in the app.

People who do not use the app

A trip can include people who never install FamSplit, such as children or grandparents. Their names, and what they owe or are owed, are entered by someone else on the trip. If your name has been added to a trip and you want it removed, ask the person who added you, or contact the address below.

Who can see your information

  • The people on your trips. Everyone who joins a trip with its invite code can see everything in that trip, including names, expenses, payments, UPI IDs and the record of changes. Only share an invite code with people you want on the trip.
  • Nobody else. Your information is not sold, and it is not shared with advertisers.

Services FamSplit relies on

FamSplit uses these companies to run. They process information on FamSplit's behalf:

  • Supabase stores accounts and trip data and runs the app's server code.
  • Google provides sign-in, delivers notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging, and hosts this website.

Information travels between the app and these services over encrypted connections. A copy of your trips is also kept on your phone so the app works without a connection.

How long information is kept

Your account and trips are kept until you delete them. Deleting a trip removes it, with its expenses and payments, for everyone on it.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time, either in the app under Account, or on the account deletion page. When you do:

  • Your sign-in and your phone's notification token are removed.
  • Trips that only you are on are deleted.
  • Trips you share with other families stay for them, and the name you used on those trips stays visible to them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
