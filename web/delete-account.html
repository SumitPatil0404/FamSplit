Delete your FamSplit account

Sign in with the Google account you use in FamSplit to permanently delete your account. You can also do this in the app under Account.

  • Your sign-in is removed for good.
  • Trips only you are on are deleted, with their expenses and payments.
  • Trips you share with other families stay for them, with your name still shown on those trips.
  • This cannot be undone.
